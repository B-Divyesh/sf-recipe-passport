## First-screen read-aloud check

“Move your recipes into a private cookbook. For cooks leaving an app or
cluttered recipe page, it keeps a searchable copy on this device. Try it with
sample data; the sample opens a ready-made cookbook.” This states the job,
audience, first action, and result in one short read. Browser tests at 390 × 844
and 1440 × 900 keep that content and all three facts above the fold.
